Five days ago, the policeman told him, the bicycle was picked up in a small village four hundred miles away.

找到车 → 写信 → 邮寄几天 → Dan 收到信（上周二） → 担心一周（7天） → 昨天去警局。也就是说，车被找到的时间应该 早于上周二至少 3–5 天。所以 “five days ago” 这个设定太短。two weeks ago / ten days ago 较合理！

call on sb. 拜访，探望
  • Have you called on George recently?
  • I called on George yesterday.
call (sb.) up 给某人打电话（美语）
  • If you want my help, just call up.
  • She will call you up tomorrow.
call at +地点 对（某个人家或地方）进行短暂访问
  • He was asked to call at the station.
call out 大声叫喊
  • Some people on the bank called out to the man in the boat.
  • .
call off 取消（某项活动）
  • For some reason, they have called off the party/the meeting.
  • It began to rain so we called off the match.
call in 召集
  • Call in an architect or engineer to oversee the work. 找一名建筑师或工程师来监督这项工程。
  • We had to call in a plumber to unblock the drain.我们只得叫个管子工来疏通下水道。
