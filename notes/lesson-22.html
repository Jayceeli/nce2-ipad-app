语法知识 动词与介词搭配的词组

更新于:2026-03-22 12:04:57
动词+介词的固定短语通常可分开使用，可以有自己的宾语，并且大部分都可用于被动结构。

on prep.在…之上

动词 + on 含义 例句
act on 遵守 He always acts on his principles.
base on 在...基础上 The decision was based on thorough research.
comment on 评论 She commented on the new book.
concentrate on 集中 Let's concentrate on the task at hand.
congratulate on 祝贺 They congratulated him on his promotion.
consult on/about 商量 We need to consult on this matter.
count on 指望，依赖 You can always count on her for support.
decide on 决定 They decided on a date for the meeting.
depend on 取决于，依赖 The success of the project depends on careful planning.
economize on 节约 We need to economize on our expenses.
embark on 从事 She is about to embark on a new career path.
experiment on 尝试 The scientists are experimenting on a new drug.
insist on 坚持 He always insists on doing things his way.
lean on (against) 倚靠于 She leaned on the wall for support.
live on 靠...为生 He lives on a modest income.
operate on 起作用（做手术） The surgeon will operate on the patient tomorrow.
perform on/in 扮演 She performed in many plays during her acting career.
pride (oneself) on 为...感到自豪 They pride themselves on their excellent customer service.
rely on 依赖、依靠、信任、相信或期望 I rely on her to get the job done.
vote on 对...表决(投...的票) They will vote on the new proposal next week.
write on/about 写...的事 She wrote an article on the benefits of exercise.

in prep.在…内

动词 + in 含义 例句
believe in 信仰 She believes in the power of positivity.
delight in 喜欢 He delights in playing the piano.
employ in 从事 They are employed in the IT industry.
encourage in 鼓励 We encourage in pursuing your dreams.
engage in 正做 He is currently engaged in research.
experience in 在...有经验 She has experience in project management.
fail in 没有尽到 I failed in completing the task on time.
help in/with 帮助 Can you help me in moving this furniture?
include in 包括 The package includes a free gift.
indulge in 沉醉 Don't indulge in excessive drinking.
instruct in 教导 He has been instructed in the right way.
interest in 对...感兴趣 She is interested in learning new languages.
invest in 投资 They decided to invest in real estate.
involved in 卷入 He was involved in a car accident.
persist in 坚持 She persists in pursuing her dreams.
share in 分享 They invited me to share in their success.

from prep. 从…起

动词 + from 含义 例句
borrow from 从...借 He borrowed a book from the library.
defend from/against 保护免于 The shield defended him from the attack.
demand from/of 向...要求 They demanded an explanation from the manager.
differ from 与...不同 His opinion differs from mine.

emerge from 从...出现 The sun emerged from behind the clouds.

hinder from 阻止 Bad weather hindered them from reaching their destination.

prohibit from 不准许 Smoking is prohibited from this area.
protect from/against 保护免于 The armor protects him from enemy attacks.
receive from 收到来自于... She received a gift from her friend.
separate from 分开 The two groups were separated from each other.
