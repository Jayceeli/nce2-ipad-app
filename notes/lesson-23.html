课文理解 surprise 意想不到的事

更新于:2026-03-13 10:02:35

I had a letter my sister yesterday. 一般过去时

She lives in Nigeria. 一般现在时

She said that she would come to England . 过去将来时

If she comes, she will get a surprise. 条件句：主将从现

get a surprise 感到惊奇
（这里 surprise 是可数名词，指“令人惊奇的事，意想不到的事”）		 to one's surprise 令某人惊讶的 = I get a shock. 吃了一惊
  • It’s a surprise to me that they can’t sell their flat.
    surprise 也可以作不可数名词，表示“惊讶”
  • She looked at the man in surprise.
  • To my surprise, the twins have nothing in common.
    令我惊讶的是，这对双胞胎毫无共同之处。 
  • I got a shock.我感到震惊。
  • I got a shock when I saw the bill.我看到账单时大吃一惊。

We are now living in a beautiful new house in the country.正在进行时

a beautiful new house 离一个名词最近的词跟该词的关系最密切, 冠词肯定是放在最前面的

  • a spare old cloth 不穿的旧衣服
  • a big red flag 大红旗

语法知识 There be 句型

更新于:2024-01-10 07:21:39
在说明或询问人或物等的存在时可用 结构 。There be 其实是“be there 的倒装”: A girl  is there in the classroom.  → There is a girl in the classroom. 表客观存在(有)。用于强调、陈述事实或介绍场景。

There be + n.＋介词短语 (出现频率 90%)

一般现在时 一般过去时
  • There is a girl in the classroom.
  • There’s a bus coming, but it’s full. (There be + n. + doing)
  • There is a girl crying in the classroom. (There be + n. + doing + 介词短语)
  • There’s a man at the door. It’s the postman. 用 it 或人称代词作进一步说明
  • There was a girl in the classroom yestoday.
  • There were some books on the table.
  • There were some people rowing on the river.
  • .
  • .

it 作为“虚主语”表示时间、距离、天气等概念时，不能用 there be 结构（因为不能还原为 be there）。

  • It’s fifteen miles to the station.

美国初中生水平

Just yesterday, a letter arrived from my sister, who's based in Nigeria. She mentioned her plans to journey to England next year. If she indeed makes the trip, she's in for quite a surprise. We're now settled in a stunning new country home. Construction kicked off before her departure, and it was finished five months back. In my reply, I extended an invitation for her to stay with us. The house boasts spacious rooms and a delightful garden. Being a strikingly modern structure, it raises eyebrows among some locals. It's likely the sole contemporary dwelling in the area.

美国口语化表达

Yesterday, a letter landed on my doorstep, penned by my sister residing in Nigeria. She shared her intention to cross the pond to England next year. Should she follow through, she'll be in for a real treat. We've since moved into a gorgeous, brand-spanking-new house nestled in the countryside. The groundwork had been laid before she took her leave, and the house was wrapped up five months prior. I dropped her a line, inviting her to bunk with us. The house is chock-full of roomy chambers, and there's a garden that's a sight for sore eyes. It's a cutting-edge pad, so it turns heads in these parts. It must be the only modern construction for miles around.

口语化词汇：“landed on my doorstep”、“cross the pond”、“brand-spanking-new”、“bunk with us”、“chock-full”、“sight for sore eyes”、“cutting-edge pad”、“turns heads”、“for miles around”。

模仿《新概念》体系

I received a letter from my sister yesterday; she currently resides in Nigeria. In her letter, she expressed her intention to travel to England next year. If she does come, she will be pleasantly surprised. We are now living in a beautiful, newly built house in the countryside. Construction started before my sister left, and the house was finished five months ago. In my response, I informed her that she is welcome to stay with us. The house features many large rooms, and it has a lovely garden. Because it is a very modern house, it appears unusual to some people. It is likely the only modern house in the entire district.

  • 更高级的词汇“currently resides”、“expressed her intention”、“pleasantly surprised”、“features”。
