语法知识 Can & Be able to

更新于:2024-02-16 02:53:54
can/could 情态动词 Can/Could I borrow you pen?表示请求别人允许或答复时的情况
can/could 表推测 I can have lunch with your tomorrow if you like.可以表示可能性
can/could 天生的或学到的能力
  • I could drive a car by the time I was sixteen.过去时
  • I tried again and found I could/was able to swim.过去时
  • Can Jane swim yet?现在时
  • No, but she’ll be able to swim in a few months’ time. 表示将来的“能力”时，则用 will be able to
正在发生的事时 Look! I can stand on my head.
表示成功地完成某一具体动作时
  • It’s pity he couldn’t visit Mary. 如果表示某一动作没有取得成功，则可用 couldn’t
  • Oh, didn’t you know? He was able to visit her after all. 通常不用 could，而用 be able to
  • He was able to leave Europe before the war began. 他在战争开始之前得以离开欧洲。
  • He was able to go to London yesterday and he enjoyed himself very much.
过去某一具体活动时
  • Could he borrow a car? 可用 could
  • Yes, he was able to borrow a car after all./No, he couldn’t. 但回答是肯定的时候则不能
