Tim never gets up early on Sundays and sometimes stays in bed until lunchtime. Last Sunday, he got up very late and noticed it was dark and raining outside. Just then, his aunt Lucy called from the station to announce that she had just arrived by train and was coming to see him. When Tim mentioned that he was still having breakfast, Aunt Lucy was extremely surprised. She informed him that it was already one o'clock in the afternoon and expressed her astonishment at his habit of getting up so late.

正在进行时

“现在”可以理解为狭义的现在与广义的现在，区分为此刻与现阶段。现在进行时常与 now，just，still 等副词连用。

表示将来确定要发生的事

  • I am coming to see you.
  • We are arriving at ....
  • The old man is dying.
句型 现在进行时
陈述句 He is listening. You are drinking. She is walking.
否定句 He is not listening. You are not drinking. She is not walking.
一般疑问句 Is he listening? Are you drinking? Is she walking?
特殊疑问句 What is he doing? What are you doing? What is she doing?
在英语中，动词 "be" 在现在进行时的口语中通常缩写为 "'s"。例如，"He is listening" 可以缩写为 "He's listening"。书面语通常。
