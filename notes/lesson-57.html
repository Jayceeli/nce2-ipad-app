语法知识 使役动词

make 和 let 后面都跟不带 to 的不定式，但意义上和用法上有区别。
make sb. do 让(命令)带有一定的“强迫，迫使”的意思 let sb. do 让(建议)提出建议、允许
She made the assistant bring almost everything in the window. Let's take a taxi. Let's go.
The teacher made the boy write the exercise again. Don' t let the children touch anything in th room please
make + 名词/代词 + do 迫使，致使(不带 to) let+名词/代词 + do 允许(不带 to)
  • She enjoyed herself making the assistant bring almost everything in the window.
  • What made him change his mind?
  • That beard makes you look much older than you really are.
  • Don't let him persuade you. 不要让他把你说服了.
  • Let me try. 让我试一拭.
  • Please let us have more time, will you?如果宾语是 us，则不能缩写成 let’s
  • He let it be known that the house was his.后面可以跟一个被动的、不带 to 的不定式
be + made 被动语态含有"迫使，致使"必须带 to let 祈使、建议、(大家一起)采取某一行动
  • He was made to work fourteen hours a day.
  • He was made to change his mind.
  • Let’s not waste any more time. 否定：“Let’s not…”
  • Don’t let’s waste any more time. 否定：“Don’t let’s…”
  • We were not allowed to speak.一般不用于被动意义来表示“被允许”，这时可用 allow

Verbs of Perception 感官动词（see, hear, watch, notice, feel 等）

  • I saw him cross the street.我看见他穿过了马路。（强调看到全过程或动作已结束）
  • We heard her sing in the next room.我们听见她在隔壁房间唱歌。
  • I noticed a man enter the building suspiciously.我注意到一个男人鬼鬼祟祟地进入了大楼。

Causative Verbs 使役动词（make, let, have）

  • My boss made me work overtime again.老板又让我加班了。
  • Please let me know if you change your mind.如果你改变主意，请告诉我。
  • I’ll have my secretary call you later.我会让我的秘书稍后给你打电话。

Semi-modal Phrases 半情态短语（had better, would rather）

  • You had better tell the truth.你最好实话实说。
  • I would rather stay at home than go out.我宁愿待在家里也不愿出去。
  • We had better not wake the baby.我们最好别吵醒宝宝。

The "But/Except" Structure do nothing but/except 结构（当前面有 do 时，后面省 to）

  • She did nothing but cry all day.她整天除了哭什么也没干。
  • I had no choice but to wait.注意：若前面没有 do，to 必须保留。
  • They did nothing except complain about the food.他们除了抱怨食物之外什么也没做。

The Flexible "Help" help 的特殊性（可带 to 也可以不带）

  • Could you help me carry these bags?你能帮我提这些包吗？（省略 to 更口语化）
  • Exercise helps reduce stress.运动有助于减轻压力。
  • He helped to clean the kitchen.他帮忙清理了厨房。（带 to 也没错，但略显正式）

The "Why" Questions Why / Why not 引导的疑问句

  • Why waste money on things you don't need?为什么要浪费钱买不需要的东西呢？
  • Why not join us for dinner tonight?今晚为什么不和我们一起吃晚饭呢？
  • Why wait until tomorrow?为什么要等到明天？

Passive Voice Trap 被动语态陷阱（还原 to 的特殊情况）

单词句型 介词复习 At, In, Off and With

在表示地点和位置时，说话人的个人角度会影响对介词的选择。
at 通常用于表示居住地点和某个停留地点、工作地点等
  • He will be at the airport to meet her.
  • I'll see you at the station. 我将到车站送你.
  • On the way home, we stopped at a small village called Puddleton.
in 表示里面或包围的含义
  • There were a lot of people in the street. 街上有许多人.
  • Let's go for a walk in the park. 让我们到公园去散散步.
He looks handsome in that uniform.表示穿着，后面加衣服或颜色
with 表示身上的某个具体特征或随身带着、拿着什么
  • That man with long hair is supposed to be a poet. 那个留着长发的男人应该是个诗人
  • The police are looking for a man with a scar on his face.
  • The woman with the brown handbag and long umbrella is a famous novelist.
  • A child came along with a brown dog.
off 常与动词连用，表示位置的变化
  • The lid came off easily. 可译为“脱掉，脱落”
  • The pencil rolled off the table. 相当于 away from 或down from 的意思
