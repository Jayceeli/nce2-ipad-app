usual adj. 通常的，平常的，惯常的 usually adv. 平常，通常
  • It is usual with him to get up late.
  • I parked the car at the usual place.
  • usual practice 惯例
  • as usual 往常一样 (As usual, he asked the same silly questions.)
  • than usual 往常相比 (Harvesting has begun earlier than usual)
  • When do you usually have supper?
  • He usually gets up at six o' clock.
  • He usually returns home very late. （注意 later 与 latter 的区别）

  • I picked up the receiver between two sticky finger.
