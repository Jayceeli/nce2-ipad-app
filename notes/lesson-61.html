语法知识 将来完成进行时

更新于:2024-03-06 06:08:23
将来完成进行时(强调过程) will/shall have been + doing
  • By Friday midday, they will have been working on it for seven days.
  • They will have been preparing for a month.
  • By this time next week, I will have been working for this firm for twenty years.
  • By Friday midday, they will have been working on it for seven days.（work on sth. 关于…的工作）
将来完成时(强调结果) will/shall have + done
  • Soon the astronauts will have repaired the telescope.
  • By next Sunday, they will have finished all the preparation.
  • Workers will have completed the new roads by the end of this year.
一般将来时(不那么确定的将来) will + do They will be here tomorrow.
将来进行时(确定的将来) will be + doing They will be arriving here tomorrow.

在时间状语从句中 要用现在完成时代替将来完成时

  • By the time I’ve completed this one, I’ll have been writing for six years.到我写完这本书时，我将已经写了 6 年书了。
  • When I’ve built this one, I’ll have built five all together.
原型 (do) 过去式 (did) 过去分词 (done) 现在分词 (doing)
