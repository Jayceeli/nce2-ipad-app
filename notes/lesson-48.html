Dentists always ask questions when is impossible for you to answer.

sth. be impossible for sb. sb.不可能(办到某事)

  • It's impossible for me to be there before eight. 我在八点之前不可能赶到那里。
  • It's impossible for him to catch the bird. 他要抓住这只鸟是不可能的。
  • It's physically impossible for her to lick your elbow. 她不可能舔到你自己的肘子。
  • However, this is impossible for farmers. 然而，这对农民来说是不可能的。
  • It is impossible for you to get there in such a short time. 你在那么短的时间内到达那是不可能的。
形式主语 主语从句
It is impossible for him to help you. It is impossible that he will help you.
To help you is impossible for him. 还原主语 He will help you is impossible. ×

for a while 一段时间

  • The music soothed her for a while. 音乐让她稍微安静了一会儿。
  • Let's talk for a while. 咱们聊一会儿吧。

how my brother was (宾语从句) How was my brother? 特殊疑问句
并列句 主语相同可以省主语、主谓相同可以省主谓、甚至主谓宾相同也可以省掉。 He then asked me how my brother was and （He asked me）whether I liked my new job in London.
