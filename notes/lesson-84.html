扩展知识 形式宾语it & 真正的宾语to do或从句.

更新于:2024-03-24 12:16:20
主语 + 动词 + 宾语 + 宾语补足语 句型中，常用 it 作先行宾语，真正的宾语为后面的动词不定式或从句。

使用条件

  1. 结构：主 + 谓 + 宾 + 宾补
  2. 宾语过长：影响到了补语，所以使用it做为形式宾语占位置。

常用这种结构的动词有：find，think，consider，feel，make，keep 等：

  • People are going to find it difficult to get to work. 形式宾语it，真正的宾语to do
  • She felt it right for them to arrest the thief.
  • They consider it impossible to finish the work in three days.
He thought passing the examination easily. 乄 He thought it easy to pass the examination. ✓ 他（过去）以为通过考试很容易
We find learning English well is very important. 乄 We find it very important to learn English well. ✓ 我们发现学好英语很重要
I feel it right that he should be punished. (to do 换从句) 我认为他应该受到惩罚是对的。
They know it impossible that they will win the match. 他们知道他们赢得比赛不可能。
每日习语 One rotten apple spoils the whole barrel. 一个坏苹果会毁掉整桶苹果。
