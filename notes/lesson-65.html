Must 通常只用于现在时和将来时，其他时态则用 have to 代替。 .
Have to 比 must 往往更强调客观的要求或外在的原因 He had to change his plans, but he should be returning soon. 
Should “应该”，表示义务、责任或某个正确的行为 疑问句与否定结构则多用 should。should/ought to +have +过去分词表示过去应该做（或完成）而没有做的事。
Ought to 比 should 语气略强些，多用于肯定句 He ought to have returned by now.

  • She should/ought to have come to see me yesterday, but she forgot.

let vt. 允许，让

let off 免除 (任务或义务) I realized that having a new baby lets you off going to boring dinner parties.
 我意识到，生了个孩子你就可以不参加无聊的宴会了。
宽恕，放过（犯错误或违法的人） As he has a good record, we shall let him off this time.
引爆，释放（压力等） We always let off fireworks. 我们总是会放烟花。
let down 失望或辜负
  • I expected him to help me, but he let me down.
  • I was really let down by his broken promise.
放松或减弱 She let down her hair and relaxed after a long day at work.
放下或降低 He let down the ladder for the firefighters to climb up.
使下降 Please let down the curtain.请把窗帘放下来。
let sb. in 允许（空气、光线、水等）进入 If anyone knocks at the door when I am out,don' t let him in.
使某人或某事陷入不利的境地 His careless actions let him in for a world of trouble with his boss. (他的粗心行为使他陷入了与老板的一系列麻烦之中。)
与某人分享信息或允许其参与 It depends on how full the class is and how many additional students the professor is willing to let in.
这取决于课堂有多满，以及教授愿意让多少额外的学生加入。
let out 结束；放学；放假 After the long meeting, the chairman finally let out a sigh of relief. (长时间的会议结束后，主席终于松了口气。)
放出去 Who let the dog out.
