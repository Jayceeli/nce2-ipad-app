语法知识 将来完成时

更新于:2026-03-13 10:02:35
将来完成时 will/shall have + done By the end of next year,they will have finishd work on the new stadium.到将来某一点时间为止, 某个动作已经发生
现在完成时 have/has + done . 到现在某一点时间为止过去完成式，到过去某一点时间为止
一般将来时
除了可以用来预言将来发生的事以外，还可以表示“意愿”，如允诺、建议、请求、提议等。
  • The radio hasn’t been mended yet. Never mind! I’ll mend it for you. （允诺）
  • Will you open the door for me please! （请求）
  • Shall we go for a swim tomorrow? （建议）
将来进行时
除了表示最近或不久的将来正在进行的动作外，还可以表示计划或安排好的事。
  • A great many people will be visiting the country.
  • ?
将来完成时 常与by 和 not…till/until+表示时间的名词连用
用于表示到将来某一时刻已经完成的动作。
  • I hope they’ll have finished it in time for the journey.
  • I expect you will have changed your mind by tomorrow.
  • I will have finished it until/till tomorrow.

单词句型 完成时态总结

将来完成时 will have + done I will have worked here for 30 days by next week.

  • Look out! A bus is coming. 当心点, 公共汽车来了.
  • You should always look out when you walk across a road.
Look out of 朝外看 Don’t .
Look up 看望、查阅 I don't understand this word. I shall look it up in a dictionary.(get information from a reference book) 查阅 (从参考书中获取资料)
  • Don't forget to look me up when you return. (visit) 拜访，看望
